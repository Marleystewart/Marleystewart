## 🚀 What I'm Building

### 🩺 Nalia | Founder
**Health Technology & Care Coordination**

Nalia is a care coordination platform helping families managing ongoing or complex care keep important information organized in one place. 

<img width="1321" height="776" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-19 at 3 51 15 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fb1a2c8d-74f9-4c8d-b827-f90d6943fe1d" />


The platform brings together:
- Appointments and medication tracking
- Therapy notes and care documentation
- IEP, 504, and IFSP plans
- Document organization
- Shared care team access
- Meeting preparation and planning

**Focus:** Health Technology, Product Development, Software Engineering

🌐 [Explore Nalia](https://joinnalia.com)

### 💼 Promptly | Internship Discovery Platform
**Career Technology & Automation**

Promptly is a platform designed to simplify the internship search process by bringing opportunities from employer career pages into one place.

The platform monitors hundreds of employer websites and helps users discover relevant internship opportunities without having to search each company individually.

### 🏨 Stayline | Hospitality Technology
**SaaS & Hospitality Operations**

Stayline is a software platform designed to help motel operators manage their properties through a more organized and accessible digital experience.

The goal is to simplify everyday workflows, improve operational efficiency, and make property management easier to navigate.

**Focus:** SaaS, Hospitality Technology, Product Design

### 🖥️ Nova | macOS Desktop Companion
**Native Application Development**

Nova is a macOS desktop companion featuring an interactive pixel-style character.

Built using Swift and SwiftUI, the project explores native application development, user interaction, and desktop experiences.

**Technologies:** Swift, SwiftUI, macOS

**Focus:** Desktop Applications, User Experience, Software Development
